Genetics Project

What We Did

In our classroom project with Mr. Sanusis, we did a DNA investigation to find out who in our classroom our DNA matched. We used a process called PCR (polymerase chain reaction) to make many copies of DNA so it could be studied more clearly. Then we compared different DNA markers from classmates to see whose genetic patterns matched.

The Outcome of the experiment

The outcome of the experiment was kind of surprising. A lot of people expected that they would match DNA markers with others of the same race or ethnicity, but that didn’t really happen. Instead, the results showed that there wasn’t a strong link between race and who matched with who. This helped us understand that DNA variation is more random and that people of different backgrounds can still share similar genetic markers.

What we learned

Our class DNA data shows that out of all the matches found, only 30 were between people with the same race, while 242 were between people with different races. On average same race matches happened only 7.2% of the time while different race matches happened at 10.2%. This shows DNA patterns do not stay within racial groups
